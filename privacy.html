EVSavvy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

EVSavvy is an Android app for electric vehicle owners in India. It helps you log your charges, see what each kilometre costs, compare that with petrol, and find public chargers on a map. This page explains what the app does with your data, in plain words.

The short version
  • There is no login and no account.
  • Your vehicles and charges are saved only on your phone. We cannot see them.
  • The app asks for your location only when you tap Near me, and never saves it or sends it to us.
  • Map images come from OpenStreetMap servers. Like any website, those servers see which map area you are looking at and your phone's internet address.
  • No ads. No analytics. No tracking tools.

What you type into the app

Vehicle details (name, battery size, prices, odometer readings, warranty dates) and every charge you log are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google account as part of your normal phone backup. That is controlled by your Android settings, not by us.

Location

When you tap Near me on the Chargers tab, Android asks you for permission to use your location. The app uses it once to centre the map on you and to list the nearest mapped chargers. The location is kept in memory only while you use the map. It is not saved and is not sent to us. You can refuse the permission; everything else in the app works without it.

Map images (OpenStreetMap)

The Chargers tab shows a map. The map images (tiles) are loaded from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's tile servers over an encrypted connection. Each request tells that server which part of the map you are viewing, and includes your phone's IP address and the app's name, the same as when you open any website. If you have used Near me, the area requested is around your location. We do not receive this information. The OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy is at osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. The map is loaded only when you open the Chargers tab.

Links to Google Maps

Buttons such as Search in Maps and Navigate in Maps open the Google Maps app or website with the chosen place. From that point Google's privacy policy applies.

Excel download

Download history (Excel) builds a spreadsheet on your phone and opens the Android share sheet so you can save it or send it with an app you choose. The file goes only where you send it.

Google Play updates

The app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available and lets Google Play download it. This uses Google Play services and is covered by Google's privacy policy. We receive nothing from it.

Other connections

The app does not contact any other server. If a future version downloads updated electricity and petrol prices, that request will carry no personal data, and this page will say so.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet: to load map images and to check for updates.
  • Location (precise and approximate): only for Near me, and only when you tap it.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage statistics. The app has no analytics and no crash-reporting tools.

Children

EVSavvy is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a vehicle in the Garage tab to remove it and its charges, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
